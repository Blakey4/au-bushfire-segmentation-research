## Plan agreed (Wenjing's wording)

- **Step 0:** reproduce the results reported in the FLOGA paper (Greek study) on their
  dataset using their pre-trained model.

  best performance on that dataset.
- **Step 2:** replace the FLOGA model with SOTA segmentation algorithms (e.g. SAM2) and
  obtain improved performance.

**Two major outcomes:**
1. A fair and comprehensive evaluation of recent representative solutions on public
   datasets and on our own Australian data.
2. A better-performing boundary-detection solution for the 2019 Australian fires.
